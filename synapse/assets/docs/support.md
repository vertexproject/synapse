# Synapse Support

Information for Vertex support.

## Slack

Best effort chat based support is available through the [Synapse Slack](https://v.vtx.lk/join-slack). You can find Vertex Project analysts, engineers, and other users who can help with questions you may have.

## Service Desk

Commercial customers have access to the Vertex Support [Service Desk](https://vertexproject.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portals). This is the ideal place for customers to submit issues, modeling questions, and feature requests.
